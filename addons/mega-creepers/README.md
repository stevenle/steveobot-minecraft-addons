# Mega Creepers

A new boss, the **Mega Creeper**, waits at the heart of underground
**creeper dungeons**. Dungeon chests and the boss drop **creeperite**, a
blast-proof green stone and metal that you can build with and make tools and
armor from.

## The Mega Creeper

A creeper twice your height with 100 hearts and a boss bar. It walks at you,
and it has two attacks:

- **Explosion.** When it gets close, its fuse hisses and it swells and
  flashes for 1.5 seconds, then explodes like a big creeper (power 4). It
  does **not** die. It barely moves while it swells, so run.
- **Firework barrage.** If you keep your distance it may charge up instead,
  flashing rainbow colours. Then it launches a volley of rockets that rain
  down on every player nearby. A **red ring** marks where each rocket will
  land, so keep moving. More players, more rockets.

After each attack it **recharges**: it slumps, goes grey and dizzy, and can
barely move. That is your window.

| After | Recharge |
|---|---|
| An explosion | 2 to 7 seconds |
| A firework barrage | 6 to 25 seconds |

Outside a recharge its hide shrugs off **75%** of all damage, so hitting it
while it is charging at you does little. Bait an attack, dodge it, then hit
it while it recovers. Below 40% health it gets **angry**: a shorter fuse,
more rockets, and barrages more often.

Its explosions break blocks **only if the `mobGriefing` game rule is on**,
exactly like a creeper's. On a Realm with `mobGriefing` off they hurt but
break nothing. Creeperite blocks are blast-proof either way, so the dungeon
itself never gets blown open. It is immune to explosions (its own, TNT's,
and charged creepers'), fireworks, and falling, and it will not leave its
dungeon: if dragged more than 24 blocks from where it woke, it teleports
back.

Defeating it drops 6 to 10 creeperite ingots, raw creeperite, a pile of
gunpowder, fireworks, a creeper head, sometimes an enchanted creeperite
chestplate, sword, or pickaxe, and 120 XP. It also **unseals the vault**.

## Creeper dungeons

Dungeons are buried between about Y -40 and Y 10, roughly one in every
192x192 block area (60% of areas have one). They are placed as you explore,
in **existing worlds too**, out of sight: never closer than 24 blocks to any
player, and never through anything built. A site with player blocks, a
mineshaft, a stronghold, an ancient city, another dungeon, or too much open
cave or water is skipped.

Most dungeons have a **ladder shaft** up to a small ruin on the surface:
four broken creeperite pillars with carved creeper faces around a hole.
Climb down. Where the ground above is water or already built on, there is
no shaft and you find the dungeon by caving or mining, like a vanilla
dungeon.

Inside:

- **Entry hall**, where the ladder comes down between two creeper statues.
  Two chests.
- **A corridor with a trap.** One of the three corridors hides a pressure
  plate. Step on it and creepers appear at both ends of the corridor.
- **Two side rooms**, picked at random from four kinds:
  - **Creeper den**: gravel and moss, cobwebs, stacked TNT (careful), and a chest.
  - **Old mine**: walls thick with creeperite ore, rails, ore pillars, and a chest.
  - **Library**: bookshelves, an enchanting table, cobwebs, a chest, and a
    charged creeper.
  - **Armory**: anvil, smithing table, grindstone, weapon racks, and two
    chests of creeperite gear.
- **A secret closet** behind one of the side rooms' far walls, with a chest
  of the best loot outside the vault. Look for a 3x3 patch of cracked bricks
  with a carved creeper face above it, and mine through.
- **The arena**: a big pillared hall with a giant creeper face in the floor.
  Walking in wakes the Mega Creeper.
- **The vault**, behind a glowing sealed door in the arena's north wall.
  Sealed creeperite cannot be mined or blown up. When the Mega Creeper
  dies, the door crumbles and the vault opens: three chests, the best in
  the dungeon.

**Creepers appear when you walk into a room**, not before. Mobs placed when
the dungeon was built would despawn before anyone arrived. Each room
re-arms after 5 minutes if its creepers are gone; trap plates re-arm after
2 minutes. Creative players do not set anything off and do not wake the boss.

| Chests | What is in them |
|---|---|
| Hall, den, mine, library | Gunpowder, raw creeperite, Creeperite blocks, creeperite ingots, iron, TNT, bones, string, bread, arrows, rockets, rarely a music disc, sometimes a worn creeperite tool or armor piece |
| Armory | 2 or 3 worn, enchanted creeperite tools or armor, plus ingots, raw creeperite, iron |
| Secret closet | An enchanted creeperite piece, diamonds, emeralds, gold blocks, ingots, golden apples |
| Vault | A well-enchanted creeperite piece, plenty of ingots, Blocks of Creeperite, diamonds, emeralds, gold, golden apples, XP bottles, a rare enchanted golden apple, the creeper banner pattern |

## Creeperite

| Block or item | How you get it |
|---|---|
| **Creeperite** | The dungeon's stone. Mine it (pickaxe), or find stacks in chests. |
| **Creeperite Ore** | In dungeon walls, and lots in the old mine. Drops 1-2 raw creeperite. |
| **Raw Creeperite** | From the ore, chests, and the boss. |
| **Creeperite Ingot** | Smelt raw creeperite (or the ore) in a furnace or blast furnace. |
| **Block of Creeperite** | 9 ingots. Crafts back into 9 ingots. |
| **Polished Creeperite** | 4 Creeperite in a square makes 4. |
| **Creeperite Bricks** | 4 Polished Creeperite in a square makes 4. |
| **Cracked Creeperite Bricks** | Creeperite Bricks + gunpowder (shapeless), or smelt Creeperite Bricks. |
| **Carved Creeperite Bricks** | 2 Creeperite Bricks stacked vertically makes 2. Has a creeper face on each side. |

The stonecutter also turns Creeperite, Polished Creeperite, and Creeperite
Bricks into any of the later stages one for one.

**Every creeperite block is blast-proof** (explosion resistance 1200, like
obsidian). Build a creeper-proof house.

### Tools and armor

All nine pieces are crafted from creeperite ingots in the usual shapes
(sticks for the tools), have **2000 durability**, repair with creeperite
ingots in an anvil, and take enchantments like netherite.

| Piece | Stat |
|---|---|
| Sword | 9 attack damage |
| Axe | 8 attack damage, mining speed 9 |
| Pickaxe | 6 attack damage, mining speed 9, mines everything diamond can |
| Shovel | 6 attack damage, mining speed 9 |
| Hoe | 5 attack damage |
| Helmet / Chestplate / Leggings / Boots | 3 / 8 / 6 / 3 armor (netherite's) |

The hoe tills, the shovel makes paths, and the axe strips logs. If the game
build does not do this for custom tools, the script does it.

**Armor bonuses:**

- **Blast-proof.** Each creeperite piece you wear cuts explosion damage by
  12.5%. The full set halves it, which helps a lot against the Mega Creeper.
- **Volatile.** With the **full set** on, a hit from a mob (or a mob's
  arrow) has a 1 in 5 chance of making **you explode** like a creeper
  (power 3), at most once every 5 seconds. The blast hurts the mobs around
  you. You, other players, and tamed pets take no damage from it, and it
  never breaks blocks, so it is safe at home.

## Chat commands

Chat is the only console on a Realm, so the add-on answers to a script
event:

```
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers give       # creeperite tools, armor, 32 ingots, 16 raw, 16 of each block
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers status     # nearest dungeon, boss state and health, armor pieces
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers build      # build a dungeon around you; you stand in the entry hall, arena to the north
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers boss       # spawn a Mega Creeper 6 blocks in front of you
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers fireworks  # make the nearest Mega Creeper fire a barrage now
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers explode    # set off your creeperite armor
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers unseal     # open the nearest dungeon's vault
/scriptevent steveo:megacreepers rearm      # the nearest dungeon's boss wakes again next time (after a defeat)
```

`build` overwrites whatever is around you without checking, so use it in a
test world. `status` also tells you where the nearest dungeon is, which
gives the game away; it is a debug tool.

## Troubleshooting

- **No dungeons anywhere**: run `status`. "0 known" after exploring a few
  hundred blocks means the generator is not running (check the version
  triple in the root `CLAUDE.md`), or every site was rejected. Sites are
  rejected for being too open, too wet, or near anything built.
- **The boss stands still and never attacks**: the script is not running;
  same check.
- **The boss does not flash, swell, or slump**: the resource pack is not
  rendering its `steveo:state` property. Bump the RP version and
  re-upload; Realm clients cache old packs.
- **The vault never opened**: the boss died while the vault's chunks were
  unloaded. Walk back into the arena and it opens, or use `unseal`.
- **A chest is empty**: its loot table failed to load; please report it.

## Not yet verified in-game

floor plan. Things to check first:

- Natural dungeon generation while exploring (`status` should count up), and
  whether sites are rejected too often.
- The ladder's `facing_direction` (2) in the entry hall. In the shaft the
  ladder has walls on all sides, so it holds either way.
- Chest facing (`minecraft:cardinal_direction`) and that chests fill from
  the loot tables through `LootTableManager`.
- The boss's look: model scale, swell, rainbow charge, grey recharge, and
  the walk speed with `melee_attack` at `reach_multiplier` 0.
- That creeperite tools lose durability on use, and armor when hit.
- Mining speed of creeperite blocks with a pickaxe (`seconds_to_destroy` 3,
  pickaxe tags).
